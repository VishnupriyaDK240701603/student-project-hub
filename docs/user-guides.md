# User Role Operating Guides

**Student Project Hub**

---

## 1. Student Guide

### Finding & Joining a Team
1. Navigate to the **Requests Feed** (`/requests`).
2. Filter requests by department or search for specific technical skills (e.g., "React", "Python", "Robotics").
3. Click **View Details** on an open request.
4. If your year and department match the lead's eligibility criteria, click **Apply**.
5. Provide a short statement of interest and attach your resume if required.

### Participating in a Project Room
1. Once accepted by the project lead, access the room under **Rooms** (`/rooms`).
2. Use **Real-time Team Chat** to communicate with teammates.
3. Track progress on the **Kanban Board** (drag & drop tasks across Backlog, In Progress, In Review, and Done).
4. Tag `@ai` in chat for automated project breakdowns and milestone schedules.

---

## 2. Team Lead Guide

### Creating a Project Request
1. Go to `/requests` &rarr; click **"Create Request"**.
2. Enter project title, description, roles needed, headcount (max 5 per team), and eligibility filters (departments, batch years, gender preference).
3. Set **Resume Requirement** toggle if candidate resumes are needed.
4. Publish the request (maximum 3 active requests per lead).

### Reviewing Applicants & Room Activation
1. View received applications under **Inbox** (`/inbox`).
2. Accept matching applicants until headcount is filled.
3. Click **"Convert to Room"** to launch the private team workspace.
4. Manage team members, invite faculty mentors, or swap lead duties from Room Settings.

---

## 3. Faculty Mentor Guide

### Accepting & Mentoring Teams
1. Access the **Mentor Console** (`/staff/mentor-inbox`).
2. Review pending invitations from student teams requesting your mentorship.
3. Accept the request to gain read-and-advise access to the project room.
4. Provide feedback in room discussions, monitor milestones, and review submitted deliverables.

---

## 4. Moderator & Staff Console Guide

### Handling Reports & Appeals
1. Open the **Moderator Console** (`/staff/moderator-console`).
2. Review reported messages, files, and users.
3. View the automatic snapshot captured at the time of the report (including context).
4. Take moderation action (Dismiss, Warning, or Permanent Account Block).
5. **Two-Moderator Appeal Consensus**: Unblocking an appealed user requires approval from **2 independent moderators**.
